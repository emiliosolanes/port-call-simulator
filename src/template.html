About this model

Personal project by emilio.solanes. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the companies mentioned.

A discrete event simulation of 48 hours at Hutchison Ports ECT Delta on Maasvlakte I. Vessels, pilots, the pilot helicopter, tugs, mooring crews, quay cranes, AGVs and stacking cranes share one event engine. The engine runs once per disruption, so every revised estimate comes from the same model as the actuals.

  • Service codes, event types (ARRI, DEPA, STRT, CMPL), phases (INBD, ALGS, OUTB) and classifiers (EST, REQ, PLN, ACT) follow DCSA Port Call 2.0. Senders and receivers follow its use cases.
  • MSC Gülsün and MSC Irina are real MSC vessels; Lion and Britannia are MSC services that call Rotterdam. Their particulars are public. Which terminal and berth they use, schedules, voyages, moves and all timestamps are simulated.
  • Feeders Nordic Tern, Humber Gull, Baltic Wren and Kattegat Eider are fictional.
  • The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap: in through the Maasmond, down the Beerkanaal, swing in the turning basin and astern into the Amazonehaven. The view is rotated so the quay runs across the screen, and offshore distances along the Maasgeul are compressed. Deep-draught vessels take the pilot by helicopter before the Eurogeul; others board at Maascenter.
  • ECT Delta as Hutchison Ports publishes it: 272 ha and 4.0 km of quay on the Europahaven and Amazonehaven, 16.65 m water depth, separate feeder and barge facilities, two rail terminals. The stacking crane blocks are drawn where OpenStreetMap maps their rails.
  • The ULCS quay on the Amazonehaven gets 11 remote-operated cranes: 8 delivered in 2014 and 2015 and 3 in 2025. Crane count per quay, 23 gross moves per hour, 6 AGVs per crane and the 3 cranes at the Delta Barge Feeder Terminal are assumptions. The western end of the quay is shown but not modelled.
  • ATA berth is first line ashore. All fast follows when mooring completes.
  • Scope: one terminal. The rest of the port is not modelled, so there is no competition from other terminals' calls for pilots, tugs, linesmen or the fairway. The Port of Rotterdam handled 27,384 seagoing calls in 2025.
  • Not modelled: SHORE_POWER, SAFETY, ANCHORAGE_OPERATIONS, shifting between berths, cancel, omit and decline.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

Sources: DCSA Port Call 2.0 use cases, Port Call 2.0 API, Hutchison Ports ECT Delta, ECT Delta cranes 2014 and 2015, ECT Delta cranes 2025, MSC at ECT Delta, MSC Lion and Britannia rotations, MSC Gülsün, MSC Irina, Maascenter, Rotterdam calls in 2025.
